5. International transfers

DockBound is operated from the United States. If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, your personal data is transferred to and processed in the US. These transfers are protected by:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under the Google Cloud Data Processing Agreement
  • Stripe: SCCs under the Stripe Data Processing Agreement (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd for EU; Stripe Inc. for UK)
  • SendGrid / Twilio: SCCs under the Twilio Data Protection Addendum

6. Cookies and tracking

DockBound does not currently use third-party tracking cookies or analytics scripts. We use Firebase Authentication (a first-party session cookie) to keep you logged in. No advertising cookies are set.

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this notice and (for EU/UK visitors) seek consent before setting non-essential cookies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

RightWhat it meansHow to exercise
Access Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you Email amy@dockbound.app with the subject line "Privacy Request — [your name]." We will respond within 30 days.
CorrectionAsk us to correct inaccurate data
ErasureAsk us to delete your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations)
PortabilityReceive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (GDPR Art. 20)
RestrictionAsk us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved
ObjectObject to processing based on legitimate interests

If you are located in California: you have the right to know, delete, and opt out of sale (we do not sell data). Submit requests to amy@dockbound.app.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); in the EU, your national supervisory authority.

8. How we protect your data

We use the following measures to protect your personal data:

  • Firebase Authentication with access controls enforced at the database level (Firestore Security Rules)
  • All data in transit encrypted via TLS; all data at rest encrypted by Firebase/Google Cloud
  • Admin functions require elevated authentication and role verification
  • Third-party API keys and service credentials stored in a dedicated secrets manager — not in application code
  • Automated retention enforcement removes data that has exceeded its retention period

9. Children

DockBound is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have collected data from a child in error, please contact us at amy@dockbound.app and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or by a prominent notice in the app at least 30 days before the change takes effect. The effective date at the top of this notice shows when it was last updated.

Notice B — Business Privacy Notice

For travel advisors and agencies using Compass, Chartroom, or Helm · Publish at dockbound.app/privacy/advisors

Effective date: [TBD — to be set on publication] · Last reviewed: September 2026

This notice is for travel professionals — individual advisors and agency staff — who use DockBound's Compass, Chartroom, or Helm products. It describes how PandA Ventures, LLC ("PandA Ventures," "we," "us," or "our") processes your personal data in the context of your professional use of the platform.

If you are a traveler using DockBound through your advisor, please see our Consumer Privacy Notice instead.

Note on traveler data you manage through DockBound When you upload your clients' data (names, emails, cruise details) to DockBound to manage their port guides, we process that data as a data processor on your behalf — you remain the data controller for those travelers. This means your own obligations to your clients (disclosure, lawful basis, consent where required) are separate from this notice. Contact us at amy@dockbound.app for a Data Processing Agreement if your clients are in the EU or UK.

4. Sharing with your agency

If you are enrolled under an agency (Chartroom or Helm plan), the agency administrator can see your name, account status, last active date, Travefy connection status, and activity summary in the Agency Hub. The agency cannot see your individual client traveler data or the content of your guide customisations.

If an agency invites you using a sponsored contract, the agency's commercial and billing configuration is used for your account. We share with the agency that you are enrolled and active — not your individual usage detail.

5. Sub-processors

We use the same sub-processors as for the consumer product (Google/Firebase, Stripe, SendGrid, Twilio), plus Travefy (OAuth token exchange). All sub-processors are subject to data processing agreements. International transfers from the EEA/UK to the US are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Your rights

You have the same rights as described in the Consumer Privacy Notice (§7): access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction, and the right to object. Submit requests to amy@dockbound.app. We will respond within 30 days.

Note: If your account is associated with an agency, erasure of your account will also remove your entries from the agency roster. We will notify you of any implications before proceeding.

7. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

If you process the personal data of travelers who are in the EU or UK, you may require a DPA with PandA Ventures covering our role as data processor for that traveler data. Contact us at amy@dockbound.app to request a DPA.
